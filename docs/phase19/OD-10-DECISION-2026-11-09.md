## 7. Findings (adopted)

1. **"Accepted with finding" is not a Passed result.** It counts towards an exit criterion only if the owner's acceptance names that criterion explicitly.
2. **Each finding records:**
   - the finding: how the observation differed from "Expected";
   - its impact;
   - the evidence: files, SHA-256 hashes, runs;
   - the risk;
   - the owner's explicit acceptance, with its date and exact wording;
   - any required remediation or follow-up, with its trigger;
   - the criterion it is accepted against.
3. **Authority:** final acceptance rests with the owner. Claude Code may propose wording but never records an acceptance itself.
4. **Not retroactive:**
   - device cases M6, M7 and M8 stand as recorded;
   - M6's acceptance (2026-11-07) names no criterion, so whether it counts towards release milestone M2 needs the owner's explicit statement, or a re-run.
